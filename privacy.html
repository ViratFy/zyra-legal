Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zyra may process limited information when you use the bot on Discord. Zyra only aims to use information that is reasonably necessary to provide its features.

1. Information Zyra may process

Depending on which features are used, Zyra may process or store limited Discord-related information such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, server configuration, moderation records, AFK data, ticket information, giveaway data, saved embeds, reaction-role settings and similar feature configuration.

2. How information is used

This information is used to operate, maintain and improve Zyra's features, remember server settings, provide requested functionality and help keep the service reliable. Zyra does not sell stored user or server data.

3. Data retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain the service, or meet legitimate operational requirements. Data that is no longer required may be removed.

4. Data deletion and questions

Server administrators or affected users may request deletion of applicable stored information or ask privacy-related questions by contacting the Zyra team through the bot's official support server or contact channel listed with the bot.

5. Account credentials

Zyra does not ask users to provide their Discord password and does not intentionally store Discord account passwords or authentication credentials.

6. Third-party platform

Zyra operates through Discord, so use of Discord itself is also subject to Discord's own policies and terms. Zyra is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Zyra's features or data practices change. The latest version published on this page will apply from its stated update date.

By continuing to use Zyra, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
